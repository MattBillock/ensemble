# Achievement System Consolidation and Expansion Requirements

## Objectives
1. Identify and remove duplicate achievements across all categories
2. Balance achievement rarity within each category
3. Add two new movie-themed categories:
   - Robin Hood: Men in Tights
   - Blazing Saddles

## Detailed Requirements
- Perform a comprehensive audit of existing achievements
- Ensure each category has a roughly equivalent distribution of rarities
  - Rare achievements
  - Epic achievements
  - Legendary achievements
- Create new achievement templates for the two new movie categories
- Maintain the overall integrity and balance of the achievement system

## Constraints
- Preserve the unique character of existing achievements
- Do not reduce the overall number of achievements significantly
- Ensure new categories are thematically consistent with existing system

## Success Criteria
- No duplicate achievements remain
- Each category has a balanced distribution of rarities
- New movie-themed categories are fully integrated
- Total number of achievements remains close to original count
